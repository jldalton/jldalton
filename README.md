## JD here 👋

I am currently an Engineering Manager at https://www.invitationhomes.com/

I am currently learning Salesforce application development, TypeScript, AWS, etc.

I started programming in the Commodore 64 era, beginning with BASIC, then advancing to 6502(6510) assembly language to 
produce arcade-style and interactive fiction games for the Commodore 64, including one that earned me some money 
for [college](https://uconn.edu/), and landed me on [The Giant List of Classic Game Programmers](https://dadgum.com/giantlist/#D).

Some of my favorite games of the past include the Ultima series (especially III and IV), the Zork series, 
and the Myst/Riven series. 

I am an avid word game player (Scrabble, Wordle, etc.) and I dabble occasionally with developing a word study application.

For the past few seven year time cycles, I've developed primarily with Java, Ruby, Python, and SQL.

In my early career I programmed with C and REXX using the IBM OS/2 operating system. I once wrote a screen saver program for OS/2 
called Fracas, which included a tornado screen saver mode. I'm pretty sure it was published in at least one OS/2 Presentation Manager book.

In the beginning of the "online era", I developed, hosted, and played trivia and word games on the Prodigy Interactive Service.

I am a proponent of normalizing [tau over pi](https://www.tauday.com/).

I also favor 24-hour time.

I once decided to write a program to calculate integer square roots up to N digits. First in 6502, next in 8088, and later 
in Python. Nowadays, it has become 
[simple to do so](https://www.wolframalpha.com/input?i=the+first+512+digits+of+sqrt%285%29).

I keep the clock in my car roughly 7 minutes ahead of the local time zone. Sometimes it helps.

I occasionally think about inventing my own calendar.

Fun fact: I've contributed a couple entries in the
[Online Encyclopedia of Integer Sequences](https://oeis.org/search?q=author%3Ajdalton&go=Search)

Ask me about the custom budgeting system I made and follow, using LibreOffice.

I also like to garden, travel, hike, read, watch (sports, tv, movies), listen to music, 
and take photographs (mostly cats, butterflies, anoles, plants, and food). I occasionally sign up for 5Ks.

You can reach me at the email address in my profile.
